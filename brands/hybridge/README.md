# Hybridge brand kit

The brand kit for the AI Engineer assessment. Treat it as the brand's source of
truth for the exercise.

## What's in here

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `logo/hybridge-logo.png` | The Hybridge Dental Implants lockup, 1150 x 339, transparent background |
| `logo/hybridge-icon.png` | The square H mark, 1254 x 1254, on white |
| `fonts/InterVariable.ttf` | Inter, our typeface. Variable: weight 100 to 900, optical size 14 to 32 |
| `fonts/InterVariable-Italic.ttf` | Inter italic, same axes |
| `fonts/OFL.txt` | Inter's licence (SIL Open Font License 1.1). Free to use and ship |

## Type

- Inter for everything: headlines, body and numbers.
- Headlines in a medium weight with slightly tight letter spacing. Posts can go bolder, as in the examples.
- Sentence case. Avoid tracked capitals.

## The feel

Premium, sleek and restrained. One strong image, few words and plenty of space.
Colour only where it carries meaning. Nothing loud or crowded.
`examples/ideal-output.png` and the inspiration board show the bar.

## Logo use

- Use the files as they are. Don't stretch or crop them, and don't add effects.
- Keep other elements clear of the logo.
- This kit has no white version. For dark backgrounds, like the examples, a
  plain white knockout of the lockup is fine for this exercise.

## Not included

Photos and illustrations. Free stock images or images you generate are fine.
